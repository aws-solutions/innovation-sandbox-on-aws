---
title: Cost
---

Configure cost details for leases and organize spending by team or project.

## Cost monitoring

Turn on **Enable service cost breakdown** to see spending by AWS service in notifications and the lease cost panel. This affects future cost observations, including those for existing leases. Historical observations are unchanged.

## Cost report groups

Use **Cost report groups** to define labels that managers can assign to lease templates for cost reporting.

Turn on **Require cost report group** to require a group when a template is created or edited. Existing templates without a group are unchanged until edited.

Removing a group prevents it from being selected again. Existing templates and leases keep their assigned group.
